# Data Analytics Portfolio

Tina Orecchia · Data & Business Analyst · Brisbane, Australia

Analysis and business analysis projects built on public Australian data, covering health, energy and public policy. Each project starts from a real business question, documents how the data was validated, and states its limitations.

**Tools:** SQL · Python · Databricks · Power BI · Excel

## Projects

| Project | Question | Methods |
|---|---|---|
| [Queensland flu season forecast](qld-flu-season-forecast/) | When will Queensland's 2027 flu season peak, and what does that mean for vaccination timing in aged care? | Time-series shape analysis, backtesting, ensemble forecast, bootstrap intervals |
| [Home battery rebate and renters](home-battery-rebate-equity/) | Are renters being left behind by the Cheaper Home Batteries rebate, even at the same income level? | Multi-source joins, validation against published figures, within-group comparison, weighted regression |
| [Water fluoridation in regional Queensland](qld-water-fluoridation/) | Do councils that stopped fluoridating show more dental hospitalisations, and what else drives the variation? | Mesh block allocation, exposure reconciliation, fixed effects, variance decomposition, waiting list trends |
| [Dental GA waiting lists: business case](dental-ga-waitlist-case/) | Why are 84% of Metro South's dental GA patients past their clinical target, and what would it cost to fix? | Business case and options appraisal, root cause analysis, process mapping, requirements backlog, prototype |

## Approach

- **Start with the decision.** Every project names who would use the result and what they would do differently.
- **Validate before analysing.** Totals are reconciled against published figures, and exclusions are stated.
- **Be honest about uncertainty.** Forecasts come with intervals and backtests, and limitations are written down.
