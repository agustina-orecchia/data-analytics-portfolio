# 1. Problem statement and business case

## Problem statement

Patients on Metro South's public dental GA waiting list are waiting far beyond their clinically recommended time. At 31 December 2025, 811 of 964 people (84%) were past target, and 397 patients assessed as needing treatment within 3 months had waited more than 15 months. Most are children with extensive decay or adults with additional needs, for whom delay means more pain, more infection and more emergency presentations, and often more complex treatment once they reach theatre.

The list has stopped growing, but at the 2025 treatment rate the existing backlog will not clear without a deliberate intervention.

## Who is affected

- **Patients and families:** children in pain, missed school days, carers of adults with disability managing repeated flare-ups.
- **Emergency departments and GPs:** dental pain and facial infections presenting while patients wait.
- **Oral health service:** clinicians managing the same patients repeatedly with temporary treatment.
- **HHS executive:** the HHS carries 44% of Queensland's overdue GA dental patients, which is visible in published performance data.

## Options

**Unit cost.** One GA dental episode is priced at $5,789: the 2025-26 National Efficient Price ($7,258 per NWAU) multiplied by the inlier price weight for AR-DRG D40Z Dental Extractions and Restorations (0.7976). This is the national activity-based funding price, not Metro South's actual cost, and excludes adjustments for Indigenous status, remoteness and paediatric patients.

### Option A: do nothing

Metro South currently treats about 6.8 more patients a month than join the list. At that surplus, clearing 811 overdue patients takes around 120 months, and new Category 2 patients continue to wait well past 3 months. No direct cost; the cost is clinical risk and continued reputational exposure.

### Option B: list validation and clinical re-triage

- A waitlist officer (AO4) for 6 months contacts every patient not contacted in 90 days: confirms they still need treatment, updates details, and flags any change in clinical need.
- A senior dentist reviews every Category 2 patient waiting more than 12 months and confirms or revises the category against the statewide criteria.
- Cost: $103,572 (AO4 pay point 4) x 1.25 on-cost loading x 0.5 years = **$64,732**, equal to **11.2 GA episodes**. Clinician review time is absorbed within existing sessions (assumption to confirm).

Validation does not treat anyone. Its value is a list that reflects real demand, consistent categories, and a smaller backlog to fund in Option C.

### Option C: purchase additional GA capacity

Extra in-house theatre sessions, or outsourced lists with private day hospitals, sized to clear the overdue backlog. Inflow is held at the 2025 level and the existing 6.8/month surplus is counted.

| Clear backlog in | Extra cases per month | Uplift on 2025 throughput | Total extra cases | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 months | 60.8 | +201% | 729 | $4.22m |
| 18 months | 38.2 | +126% | 688 | $3.98m |
| 24 months | 27.0 | +89% | 647 | $3.75m |

A 200% uplift in 12 months is unlikely to be deliverable given theatre and anaesthetist availability. 18 to 24 months is the realistic range.

### Option D (recommended): B, then C sized on the validated list

| Validation removes | Clear in 18 months | Cost incl. validation | Clear in 24 months | Cost incl. validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0% | 38.2 cases/month | $4.05m | 27.0 cases/month | $3.81m |
| 10% | 33.7 cases/month | $3.58m | 23.6 cases/month | $3.34m |
| 20% | 29.2 cases/month | $3.11m | 20.2 cases/month | $2.87m |

Each 10% of overdue patients removed through validation saves about $470k of purchased capacity. Validation also gives the HHS confidence that the capacity it buys goes to patients who still need it.

## Benefits

| Benefit | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Patients treated within clinical time | % treated within target | from 23% to 70% within 24 months |
| Long waits eliminated | Patients waiting 12+ months | from 546 to under 50 within 24 months |
| Reliable list | % of list contacted in the last 90 days | 95% from month 4 |
| Sustainable flow | Treated per month / additions per month | 1.0 or higher every quarter |
| Fewer avoidable presentations | ED presentations for dental conditions among listed patients | baseline in discovery, then reduce |

Targets are proposals for discussion with the oral health service, not commitments.

## Not in scope

- Changes to the statewide GA categorisation criteria (the case recommends consistent application, not new criteria).
- Prevention programs that reduce future demand, such as school fissure sealants or water fluoridation. These matter for the long term but don't move the current backlog.
- Workforce planning for anaesthetists and theatre nursing beyond the capacity purchased.
